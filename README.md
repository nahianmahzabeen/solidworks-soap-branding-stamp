# Precision Soap Branding Stamp

A custom 3D CAD-designed stamp developed for imprinting distinctive branding onto soap bars produced for the University of Waterloo's Chemical Engineering Design Symposium.

## Project Overview

Designed and modelled a custom soap-branding stamp in SolidWorks, incorporating a raised impression and a dedicated handle for practical use during soap production.

## Engineering Design Features

* **3D CAD Modelling:** Constructed the stamp using Revolved Boss/Base, Boss-Extrude, and Extruded Cut features.
* **Micro-Vent Design:** Incorporated small holes across the stamping surface to facilitate air release and help minimize trapped air bubbles during stamping.
* **Dimensional Design:** Modelled a 50 mm diameter stamp face with a 5 mm main body and raised impression features.
* **Technical Documentation:** Produced a dimensioned engineering drawing of the finished model.
